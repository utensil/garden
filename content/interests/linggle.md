[Linggle](https://search.linggle.com/) — a web-scale linguistic search engine for
words in context: query patterns (e.g. wildcards, alternatives, POS) to find how
words and phrases are actually used. See the paper
[*Linggle: a Web-scale Linguistic Search Engine for Words in Context*](https://aclanthology.org/P13-4024.pdf).
